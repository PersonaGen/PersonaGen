## IPIP questionnaire items

The question text in `examples/bigfive_questions.jsonl` and `examples/ipip50_questions.jsonl` comes from the International Personality Item Pool's Big-Five factor markers. The IPIP project explicitly places its items, scales, and inventories in the public domain. See [permission statement](https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm), [50-item format](https://ipip.ori.org/new_ipip-50-item-scale.htm), and [scoring key](https://ipip.ori.org/newBigFive5broadKey.htm). Source wording has an introductory “I” where appropriate; the item IDs follow the software's trait-grouped schema. This item status does not apply to collected response records.

## Example personas and external inputs

The profiles in `examples/fictional_personas.jsonl` are fictional examples created for this repository. `examples/offline_demo.py` generates synthetic response vectors locally.

Users obtain external datasets and models from their providers under each provider's terms. The repository's MIT license does not grant rights to those external inputs.
